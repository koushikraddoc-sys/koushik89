Program Structure

The Hybrid Learning Model

Not just a lecture-based course. A continuous learning loop combining online theory with rigorous onsite hands-on practice.

3 Sundays Every Month

Live Online Classes

2–3 hours per session focused on teaching, case discussions, reporting approach, and interactive learning.

  • Real-world case assignments
  • Pre-read reporting opportunities

2 Days Every Month

Onsite Hands-On Training

Dedicated sessions to convert theoretical knowledge into practical procedural confidence.

  • Probe & Patient positioning
  • Live demonstrations & practice
  • Biopsies, aspirations & injections

8 Months Continuous

Personalized Mentorship

Progressive guidance. Submit reports, receive structured feedback, and refine your diagnostic reasoning.

  • Compare with expert reports
  • Identify errors & missed findings

Syllabus

What You Will Learn

1. MSK Ultrasound

Fundamentals to Advanced

  • • Principles of MSK ultrasound
  • • Probe selection & optimization
  • • Dynamic & systematic scanning
  • • Tendons, ligaments, muscles
  • • Nerves, bursae & joints
  • • Doppler applications & pitfalls

2. MSK MRI Diagnostic

Joint-by-Joint Approach

  • • Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist & Hand
  • • Hip, Knee, Ankle & Foot, Spine
  • • Systematic MRI approach
  • • Degenerative & injury patterns
  • • Cartilage & labral pathology
  • • Postoperative imaging

3. Advanced Reporting

Image → Diagnosis → Report

  • • Structured reporting format
  • • Standardized terminology
  • • Grading & severity assessment
  • • Relevant negative findings
  • • Surgical planning info
  • • Concise, useful impressions

4. Mentorship

Assignments & Feedback

  • • Report cases independently
  • • Receive expert review
  • • Identify errors / missed findings
  • • Understand reporting language
  • • Build confidence via repetition

5. Interventions

USG-Guided Procedures

  • • Joint & periarticular injections
  • • Tendon sheath & bursa injections
  • • Hydrodissection
  • • Aspiration procedures
  • • Calcific tendinopathy
  • • Ganglion & trigger-point

6. Biopsies

Image-Guided Sampling

  • • Ultrasound-guided biopsies
  • • CT-guided biopsies
  • • FNAC / aspiration
  • • Soft-tissue & MSK targeting
  • • Safe needle trajectory
  • • Avoidance of neurovascular structures

7. Pain Management

Image-Guided Relief

  • • Peripheral nerve blocks
  • • Facet & SI joint interventions
  • • Epidural procedures
  • • Selective nerve-root blocks
  • • Perineural injections
  • • Imaging → Planning → Injection

8. Spine Interventions

Diagnostic & Interventional

  • • Spine imaging correlation
  • • Facet joint procedures
  • • Epidural interventions
  • • Image-guided injections
  • • Needle trajectory planning
  • • Safety & complication avoidance

Pedagogy

Case-Based Learning Approach

Every online session includes real-world case-based teaching. This helps develop diagnostic reasoning rather than memorization.

Clinical History MRI/USG Review Identify Pathology Discuss Differential Write Report Expert Pearl

  • Radiologists seeking structured, continuous MSK training and mentorship.
  • Radiologists aiming to develop robust MSK ultrasound skills.
  • Practitioners planning to start an MSK interventional practice.
